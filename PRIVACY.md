# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how StockLookUp (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our SMS alert service.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

* Phone number (for sending SMS alerts)
* Messaging preferences (opt-in/opt-out status)
* Basic usage data (e.g., delivery status)

We do **not** collect sensitive personal information such as financial account details.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

* Send stock trading alerts via SMS
* Respond to support requests
* Maintain and improve the service

## 4. SMS Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from us.

* Message frequency varies
* Message and data rates may apply
* You can opt out at any time by replying **STOP**
* You can get help by replying **HELP**

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not sell, rent, or share** your personal information with third parties.

We may use third-party services (such as SMS providers) solely to deliver messages.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

## 8. Your Rights

You can:

* Opt out of SMS at any time (reply STOP)
* Contact us to request deletion of your data

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of changes.
