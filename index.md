# Stock Alert Service

We provide stock trading alerts based on volume concentration signals and market data analysis.

## How It Works

Users opt in by providing their phone number and receive SMS alerts about stock opportunities.

## SMS Program Details

* Message frequency varies
* Message and data rates may apply
* Reply **STOP** to unsubscribe at any time
* Reply **HELP** for help

## What You Get

* Alerts based on unusual trading activity
* Signals derived from volume concentration patterns
* Daily and intraday opportunities (when applicable)
